## Guidance

Hover Card shows rich preview content when the pointer rests on a trigger. `variant` is an emphasis ranking, and this component has only `default`. It does not carry status.

<Canvas of={HoverCardStories.Default} />

## When to use / When not to use

Use Hover Card for profile previews, link unfurling, or supplemental detail that should appear on hover with a short delay.

Do not use Hover Card for click-driven panels (Popover), menus (Dropdown Menu), or content that must stay open without hover (Popover or Dialog).

## Do / Don't

**Do**

- Tune `openDelay` and `closeDelay` on `HoverCard` when the default 700ms / 300ms feels sluggish or twitchy.
- Keep the preview concise. The default width is 320px (`w-80`).
- Use `HoverCardTrigger` with `asChild` on links when the trigger is inline text.

<Canvas of={HoverCardStories.AsChild} />

**Don't**

- Don't put essential actions only inside the hover card. Keyboard users may never open it.
- Don't use Hover Card for form fields that require focus inside the panel.
- Don't remove the border or shadow; the measured surface matches Popover elevation.

<Canvas of={HoverCardStories.States} />

## Accessibility in practice

The trigger must be focusable. Radix opens the card on focus as well as hover. Escape dismisses the open card. The content is not modal and does not trap focus.

Provide meaningful link text on the trigger. The card body does not automatically become the accessible name of the trigger.

## Content guidelines

- Lead with a title line, then one sentence of supporting text.
- Mirror the trigger label in the title when previewing an entity handle.
- Avoid paragraphs longer than two lines inside the 320px width.

## Props

`HoverCard` wraps Radix Root and defaults `openDelay` to 700 and `closeDelay` to 300. `HoverCardContent` adds `variant` and `size` from `hoverCardVariants`. Radix positioning props (`side`, `align`, `sideOffset`) pass through to the content.
